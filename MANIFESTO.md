# NeXTBrain Manifesto

> *We believe classic computers deserve a future.*

Not by pretending they are modern,
but by giving them access to modern intelligence.

NeXTBrain is not an AI model.

It is not tied to a single operating system.

It is not tied to a single client.

It is not tied to a single vendor.

NeXTBrain is a modular intelligence server inspired by the philosophy that great software should be elegant, understandable, and built to last.

Intelligence changes.

Models change.

Hardware changes.

Architecture endures.

Every component should be replaceable.

Every protocol should be documented.

Every client should be welcome.

A NeXT workstation.

A Macintosh.

A DOS machine.

A UNIX terminal.

A modern laptop.

If it can communicate, it can become a NeXTBrain client.

We believe your data belongs to you.

We believe local-first is the default.

We believe software should explain itself.

We believe the best systems grow one well-tested step at a time.

We are not trying to recreate the past.

We are extending it.

**Welcome to NeXTBrain.**
